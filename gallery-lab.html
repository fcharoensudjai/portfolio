gallery lab

Five treatments of the same artwork, stacked. Scroll through, hover the ones that invite it, and tell me which ones feel alive — not which layout you like.

[ 01 ]

depth of field

the row drifts on its own. the piece nearest the centre of the screen stays sharp and in full colour; everything else falls out of focus. no scrolling required — you just exist and watch the focus travel.

[ 02 ]

resolve

each piece sits as noise — an unresolved signal — and resolves into the artwork on hover. your scramble language, applied to image instead of text. (hover a tile)

[ 03 ]

flip wall

the cover's tile-flip trick, brought into the gallery. hover a tile and a ripple of its neighbours flips over to the back. (move across the wall)

[ 04 ]

window pan

each card is only a crop. move the cursor over it and the window slides across the full canvas — you see more of the art without more space. (hover + move)

[ 05 ]

curated cut

static, asymmetric, deliberately partial — five works and an explicit "there's more" banner. the sneak peek is the count gap: you see five, the banner says how many exist.
