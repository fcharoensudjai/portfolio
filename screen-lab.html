screen lab — the recents block on a wide screen

Each band below is the same block, full viewport width, with three dials changed: reading scale (the caption), display scale (the title) and layout scale (the gutter and the column gap). Resize the window and the numbers under each label update — measure is how wide the caption actually renders, which is the thing that decides whether a paragraph still reads.
